From production line to customer

Choose a stage to see what we handle.

Goods in from your line

We receive pallets and full loads at 11 dock doors, check them against your advance notice and book them into stock within 12 hours.

  • Advance shipping notices and dock appointments
  • Unloading of trailers and containers
  • Count, quality check and photo report
  • Pallet labelling with batch and lot data

Storage that stays organised

Pallet racking, bulk floor space and shelving for cartons, with every position tracked in our warehouse system.

  • Batch, lot and serial number tracking
  • Expiry dates with first-expired, first-out picking
  • Seasonal overflow space when volumes peak
  • Regular cycle counts, reported to you

Work that finishes the product

We do the last steps between your factory and the customer, so finished goods leave ready to sell.

  • Kitting, bundling and display building
  • Relabelling, stickering and inserts
  • Repacking into retail or shipping cartons
  • Quality checks and return refurbishment

Pallets and parcels, one stock

Retail replenishment, distributor orders and single consumer parcels all ship from the same inventory.

  • Pallet and mixed-pallet shipments
  • Retail labels and delivery appointments
  • Parcels through our carrier network
  • Same-day dispatch for orders in by 2 pm

One stock, two ways out

Serve retail and consumers from the same inventory, without counting twice.

Pallets to retailers and distributors

  • Full and mixed pallets, built to retailer rules
  • Routing labels and delivery appointments
  • EDI messages or portal uploads
  • Proof of delivery sent to your inbox

Parcels to consumers

  • Orders from your own webshop and marketplaces
  • Same-day dispatch when confirmed by 2 pm
  • Branded packaging and inserts
  • Returns handled and restocked

Your systems stay in charge

We connect to your ERP so stock, orders and shipments stay in sync, with no double entry.

  • EDI and API

    Orders, stock and shipment messages in both directions.

  • SFTP and CSV

    A simple file exchange when a full integration is not needed.

  • Batch and lot tracking

    Trace any pallet back to the production run it came from.

  • Expiry control

    First-expired, first-out picking and alerts before dates run out.

  • Serial numbers

    Recorded at goods in and again at dispatch.

  • Monthly reporting

    Stock, dispatch and accuracy figures, sent to you every month.

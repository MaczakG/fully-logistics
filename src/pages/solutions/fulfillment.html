From your supplier to your customer’s door

Six stops, all run by one team.

  1. {{icon:tray-arrow-down}}

    Send your stock

    Ship goods to our dock with an advance notice. We accept parcels, cartons and full pallets.

  2. {{icon:scan}}

    We check it in

    Every delivery is counted, scanned and inspected. Any difference is reported to you the same day.

  3. {{icon:plugs-connected}}

    Orders sync in

    Orders flow in from your shop automatically, and stock levels flow back the moment something moves.

  4. {{icon:package}}

    We pick and pack

    Orders are picked in waves, packed to your specification and checked before sealing.

  5. {{icon:truck}}

    The carrier collects

    Labels are printed for the carrier you choose, and tracking links go out to your customers.

  6. {{icon:arrow-u-up-left}}

    Returns come back

    Returned parcels are inspected within one working day, and your stock updates the same day.

A price list you can read in a minute

We quote per client, but the structure never changes.

Storage
Charged per pallet or shelf position, per month. You pay for the space you actually use.
Pick and pack
Charged per order, with a small charge for each extra item. Packaging materials are itemised.
Shipping
Charged at carrier rates, using the volume discounts we have negotiated.

Extras such as kitting, inserts or relabelling are quoted per job, before we start.
